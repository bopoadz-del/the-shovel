# Scaffolds — Site Safety Criteria (OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart L)

REAL, sourced scaffold thresholds for construction sites. Source: OSHA 29 CFR
1926.451 (Subpart L, general requirements), US federal regulation, public domain.
US-reference standard: in Saudi Arabia site HSE is governed by Saudi labour law
(MHRSD) and the project HSE plan, not OSHA, but Saudi practice broadly aligns.
Use as an engineering reference; enforce against the project HSE plan and the
scaffold manufacturer's / designer's data. Values are metric-led (SI) with the
original US-customary figure in parentheses.

## Capacity and stability

| Parameter | Value (SI) | Original (US) |
|---|---|---|
| Load capacity (support own weight plus intended load ×) | 4× intended load | 4× intended load |
| Height-to-base-width ratio above which restraint (guy/tie/brace) is required | more than 4 : 1 | more than 4 : 1 |

Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.451(a)(1), (c)(1).

## Fall protection and guardrails

| Parameter | Value (SI) | Original (US) |
|---|---|---|
| Height above which fall protection is required on a scaffold | 3.05 m | 10 ft |
| Guardrail top-rail height (platforms manufactured after 1 Jan 2000) | 0.97–1.15 m | 38–45 in |
| Maximum gap between platform and uprights (normal) | 2.5 cm | 1 in |
| Maximum gap between platform and uprights (where wider is required) | 24.1 cm | 9½ in |

Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.451(g)(1), (g)(4)(ii), (b)(1).

## Minimum clearance from energized power lines

| Line voltage | Minimum clearance (SI) | Original (US) |
|---|---|---|
| Insulated, under 300 V | 0.9 m | 3 ft |
| Uninsulated / under 50 kV | 3.05 m | 10 ft |
| Over 50 kV | 3.05 m + 10 mm per kV over 50 kV | 10 ft + 0.4 in per kV over 50 kV |

Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.451(f)(6).

## KSA applicability

OSHA 1926 is a US-reference standard, not the governing authority in Saudi
Arabia (site HSE is under Saudi labour law / MHRSD and the project HSE plan).
Saudi practice broadly aligns on the 4× capacity factor, fall protection at
~3 m, and power-line clearances. Where a value is enforced on site, confirm it
against the project HSE plan and the scaffold designer's data.

## How to use

- Quote the SI value for site use; cite the US original and the regulation
  number so the basis is traceable.
- The scaffold manufacturer's / designer's rated data and the project HSE plan
  take precedence over these reference figures.
- State that this is a US-reference figure, not the governing authority for a
  Saudi project.
