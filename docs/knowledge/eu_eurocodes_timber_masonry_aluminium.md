# Eurocodes — timber, masonry and aluminium reference pointers

Citation-only navigation, checked 2026-11-06 (Asia/Dubai). Descriptions below are
original scope pointers, not copied titles, technical requirements or design
values. The [basis/action map](eu_eurocodes_basis_and_actions.md) explains the
National Annex and generation boundaries. Older base-publication maps below
must not be interpreted as a complete current national design suite.

## EN 1995 — structural timber

The family covers timber and engineered wood structural work. It occupies the
material-design layer, with actions and the shared design basis elsewhere [T1].

| First-generation citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| EN 1995-1-1:2004 | Timber members and building structures | Main timber-design entry |
| EN 1995-1-2:2004 | Timber structures in fire situations | Timber-specific fire reference |
| EN 1995-2:2004 | Timber bridges | Bridge-specific extension |

The JRC verifies the older table [T1]. BSI also verifies **BS EN 1995-3:2025**:
a second-generation timber execution reference [T2]. It sits at the
construction/workmanship interface and is not the timber bridge part.

## EN 1996 — structural masonry

This is the masonry material-design family. The older map distinguishes common
structural work, fire, the design/execution interface, and a simplified route for
unreinforced masonry. The JRC verifies all rows below [M1].

| First-generation citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| EN 1996-1-1:2005 | Reinforced and unreinforced structural masonry | Main masonry-design entry |
| EN 1996-1-2:2005 | Masonry structures in fire situations | Masonry-specific fire reference |
| EN 1996-2:2006 | Masonry material selection and construction interfaces | Link between design assumptions and execution |
| EN 1996-3:2006 | Simplified treatment of unreinforced masonry | Alternative design-method pointer; eligibility is not supplied here |

## EN 1999 — structural aluminium

This family is for aluminium structural work rather than steel. The JRC
verifies the following first-generation organization [A1].

| Citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| EN 1999-1-1:2007 | Aluminium members and structural design | Main aluminium-design entry |
| EN 1999-1-2:2007 | Aluminium structures in fire situations | Aluminium-specific fire reference |
| EN 1999-1-3:2007 | Fatigue-sensitive aluminium work | Repeated-loading reference |
| EN 1999-1-4:2007 | Formed aluminium structural sheets | Product/form-specific extension |
| EN 1999-1-5:2007 | Aluminium shells | Shell-structure reference |

## Application boundary

These are topic locators, not tables of strengths, service conditions, charring,
slenderness, mortar properties, alloy grades or member dimensions. The first-
and second-generation references are deliberately separated. Applicable national
edition, matching annex, amendments and cross-part compatibility:
**confirm against the official code**. Product standards and execution documents
remain separate from the material-design scope described here.
