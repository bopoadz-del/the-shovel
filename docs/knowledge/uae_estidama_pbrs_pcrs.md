# Estidama — Pearl Building and Community rating systems

Independently authored guidance using the official **PBRS** and **PCRS Design &
Construction manuals, version 1.0, April 2010**, plus DMT's **Information Bulletin
19, version 1.0, 27 May 2025**. Sources checked on **2026-11-06**. Credit identifiers
below belong to the specified manual; matching identifiers in the two systems can
mean different things. This summary is not a substitute for the current manuals,
bulletins, calculators, or assessor instructions. [Sources 1–3]

## Select the correct assessment boundary

**PBRS concerns buildings; PCRS concerns communities and their shared systems.**
Community assessment addresses matters such as infrastructure, public space,
movement, and requirements passed to individual buildings. A community result
does not automatically certify each building, and a building certificate does not
establish community compliance. Identify ownership, development boundaries,
phasing, use, and the rating obligation accepted by the authority. [Sources 1–2,
introductory applicability sections; Source 2, LC-R5]

Do not apply the 2010 villa guidance without checking later policy. Bulletin 19,
**Compliance Guidance 1–2 and footnote 1**, assigns applicable new stand-alone
villas above **2,000 m² gross conditioned floor area** to PBRS. Its applicability
qualification distinguishes developer/business-group ownership from private
ownership recorded on the municipal plot plan. For private owners it describes
PVRS where a DMT-approved plot regulation requires it, otherwise the temporary
Estidama programme. This is not a universal rule that every large private villa
must use PBRS. Check the bulletin's definitions, ownership category, development
controls, and subsequent instructions. [Source 3]

## Prerequisites, points, and stage gates

An **R-designated credit is a prerequisite**, not optional points that can be
traded against an unrelated improvement. Optional points support the higher
rating levels only after the required credits are met. The two 2010 manuals give
different point thresholds. Restated from their respective **Table 1, printed
page 2**: [Sources 1–2]

| Rating level | PBRS optional-point minimum | PCRS optional-point minimum |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 Pearl | Required credits suffice | Required credits suffice |
| 2 Pearl | 60 | 55 |
| 3 Pearl | 85 | 75 |
| 4 Pearl | 115 | 100 |
| 5 Pearl | 140 | 125 |

Every row also requires all prerequisites. These are verified **2010 manual**
thresholds, not a statement of the minimum rating mandated today for every
building or ownership type. The later authority statement and government-project bulletin below establish a
general policy baseline; current exceptions and the particular target still need
to be reconciled with Bulletin 19 and the authority.

The introductory rating-process sections distinguish **design assessment** from
**construction assessment**. Design intent is not proof of the completed works.
The Pearl Qualified Professional coordinates the submission; the assessor's
review/award is a separate function. Plan credit evidence through design,
procurement, installation, testing, and closeout rather than relying on a score
estimate at completion. [Sources 1–2, introductory process/roles sections and
individual credit submission requirements]

## PBRS — building-level review map

| Credit references in PBRS | Own-word review focus |
|---|---|
| **IDP-R1–IDP-R3; IDP-3; IDP-4** | Coordinate the interdisciplinary strategy, tenant fitout guidance, commissioning, construction environmental management, and envelope-performance verification. |
| **NS-R1–NS-R3** | Carry ecological/site findings into protection decisions and the ongoing management approach. |
| **LBo-R3; LBi-R1–LBi-R3** | Address outdoor comfort and indoor ventilation, smoking exposure, and water-system hygiene as separate prerequisites. |
| **LBi-2.1; LBi-2.2; LBi-3** | Connect finish-emissions evidence and construction indoor-air-quality management with the installed spaces. |
| **PW-R1; PW-R2; PW-3; PW-4** | Distinguish indoor consumption reduction, outside-water monitoring, leak detection, and runoff design; do not transfer PCRS meanings onto these identifiers. |
| **RE-R1–RE-R3; RE-6** | Demonstrate building energy performance, metering/reporting, and ozone-related requirements; separately assess any renewable-energy points. |
| **SM-R1–SM-R3; SM-9; SM-10; SM-12** | Establish restricted-material control and waste arrangements, then substantiate claimed regional/recycled/timber attributes through the actual procurement record. |
| **IP-1; IP-2** | Treat innovation claims as separately assessed opportunities, not automatic relief from prerequisites. |

A submission needs the evidence required by its credit at the appropriate stage;
a specification promise, purchase record, installation record, and test result
are not interchangeable. [Source 1, cited credits]

## PCRS — community-level review map

| Credit references in PCRS | Own-word review focus |
|---|---|
| **IDP-R1–IDP-R3** | Establish the community strategy, building-facing sustainability guidelines, and commissioning of dedicated community infrastructure. PCRS IDP-R2 is not the PBRS tenant-fitout credit. |
| **NS-R1–NS-R3** | Link the community-wide site/ecology assessment to protection and management commitments. |
| **LC-R2–LC-R5; LC-1; LC-2; LC-6** | Coordinate urban design, amenities, external comfort, building-rating interfaces, transit, connectivity, and walkability across the development. |
| **PW-R1–PW-R3; PW-2** | Set community water policy, building instructions, monitoring/leak detection, and runoff measures. PCRS PW-R1 is not the PBRS interior-fixture prerequisite. |
| **RE-R1–RE-R3; RE-3.1–RE-3.3** | Coordinate community energy policy, building instructions, monitoring, and relevant lighting/district-cooling/grid infrastructure. |
| **SM-R1–SM-R3** | Address the PCRS-specific treated-timber restriction and construction/operational waste arrangements. PCRS SM-R1 does not have the same scope as PBRS SM-R1. |

Keep community and building calculations/evidence traceable to each other without
counting a shared measure twice or implying that one assessor decision certifies
both scopes. The manuals' inter-system credit relationships identify interfaces,
not blanket equivalence between all credits. [Source 2, cited credits and
credit-relationship appendix; Source 1, credit-relationship appendix]

## Additional source-verified policy and performance examples

### Mandatory rating policy and community scope

Abu Dhabi City Municipality's **8 January 2025 statement** describes the general
permit baseline as **1 Pearl for privately funded** and **2 Pearls for
government-funded** projects. **Information Bulletin 1, 6 December 2010**, sets
out the government-project funding/public-use interpretation. Read this general
policy with the later villa-specific Bulletin 19 above; it is not evidence that
every privately owned villa has identical PBRS obligations. [Sources 3–5]

The **2010 PCRS applicability section, printed page 7**, identifies a minimum
permanent residential population of **1,000**. It describes district scale as
**20,000–30,000 residents**, with separate applications for larger developments
and defined boundaries for a component/phase. These are the reviewed manual's
scope rules, not an unsourced current planning/population waiver. [Source 2]

### Building and community credits are not interchangeable

- **PBRS RE-R1, printed pages 141–142:** the 2010 prerequisite requires at least
  **12%** improvement in annual **energy consumption**, relative to its
  **ASHRAE 90.1-2007 Appendix G** baseline. This uses **kWh**, not the model
  standard's cost comparison. Local requirements can make the baseline more
  stringent; end-use coverage, mandatory provisions and tenant assumptions remain
  part of the calculation. This historical method is not a licence to substitute
  a newer Appendix G baseline without current assessor agreement. [Source 1]
- **PBRS PW-R1, printed pages 118–119:** proposed internal potable demand must
  not exceed its calculated baseline using demand-efficiency measures; recycled
  supply cannot create the prerequisite saving. Illustrative baseline inputs are
  a private bathroom tap at **6 L/min and 417.7 kPa**, and a dual-flush toilet at
  **6/4 L per full/reduced cycle**. These are baseline-model inputs, not a
  universal product specification or Dubai's fixture schedule. [Source 1]
- **PBRS SM-R2, printed page 173**, uses at least **30%** final construction/
  demolition recycling or salvage; **PCRS SM-R2, page 139**, uses **50%** for the
  community infrastructure/public-realm scope. Each requires a pre-work plan and
  monthly monitoring. Hazardous waste is excluded; choose weight or volume and
  maintain the same measurement basis. The identical credit name does not make
  the two numerical prerequisites identical. [Sources 1–2]
- **PCRS LC-R4, Table LC-R4a, printed pages 58–59:** its **75%** pedestrian
  shading criterion is assessed over the specified **1.8 m** walking zone, with
  the prescribed equinox and summer-solstice evaluation at **13:00 UAE local
  time (Asia/Dubai)**. The area must qualify at both assessment dates; tree shade
  uses **three-year growth**. Parking, playgrounds, cycle tracks and other public
  spaces have different targets and exclusions. [Source 2]
- **PBRS SM-9, printed pages 186–187:** regional-material eligibility uses actual
  transport distance no greater than **500 km** from the furthest origin, and
  disallows any airfreight leg. The **10% / 20%** total-material-cost tiers earn
  **1 / 2 points** respectively, with the credit's cost-accounting rules still
  applicable. A local supplier address alone does not demonstrate the transport
  chain. [Source 1]

### A current planning interface is publicly documented

DMT's **18 August 2026 Detailed Master Plan guidance, D4.1.1 and D4.1.3–D4.1.6**,
requires relevant registration updates, interdisciplinary/stage handover planning,
and community water, energy and materials/waste strategies. It expressly carries
PCRS building water/energy guidelines into the development controls. This is
planning-submission evidence, not a new point threshold or automatic building
certification. See the [authority workflow](uae_authority_noc_workflow.md) for the
master-plan approval dependency. [Source 6]
