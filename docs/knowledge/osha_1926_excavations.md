# Excavations & Trenching — Site Safety Criteria (OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P)

REAL, sourced excavation and trenching thresholds for construction sites.
Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.651 and 1926.652 (Subpart P) plus Appendix B, US federal
regulation, public domain. US-reference standard: in Saudi Arabia site HSE is
governed by Saudi labour law (MHRSD) and the project HSE plan, not OSHA, but
Saudi practice broadly aligns. Note that Gulf ground conditions (sabkha,
collapsible and high-water-table soils) often demand stricter shoring than the
generic soil types below — always defer to the project geotechnical report.
Values are metric-led (SI) with the original US-customary figure in parentheses.

## Access, egress and edge protection

| Requirement | Value (SI) | Original (US) |
|---|---|---|
| Depth at which egress (ladder/ramp/stairway) is required | 1.22 m | 4 ft |
| Maximum lateral travel to egress in a trench | 7.62 m | 25 ft |
| Minimum distance to keep spoil/materials from the edge | 0.61 m | 2 ft |

Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.651(c)(2), (j)(2).

## When a protective system is required

| Requirement | Value (SI) | Original (US) |
|---|---|---|
| Depth requiring a protective system (unless in stable rock) | 1.52 m | 5 ft |
| Depth above which the system must be designed by a registered engineer | 6.09 m | 20 ft |

Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.652(a), (b); Appendix B to Subpart P. Below 1.52 m a
competent person may exempt an excavation only if examination shows no cave-in
potential.

## Maximum allowable slopes by soil type (simple slope)

| Soil type | Max slope (H:V) | Angle from horizontal |
|---|---|---|
| Stable rock | Vertical | 90° |
| Type A | 3/4 : 1 | 53° |
| Type B | 1 : 1 | 45° |
| Type C | 1½ : 1 | 34° |

Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P, Appendix B, Table B-1. Slopes are for
excavations up to 6.09 m (20 ft) deep; deeper cuts must be engineer-designed.
Soil type is set by a competent person; where types are uncertain, use the more
conservative (Type C).

## Inspection and water

- A competent person must inspect the excavation, adjacent areas, and protective
  systems before each shift, as needed through the shift, and after every
  rainstorm or other hazard-increasing event.
- Work is not permitted in an excavation with accumulated water unless adequate
  precautions (support/shielding, water removal and monitoring, safety harness
  and lifeline) are taken.

Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.651(h), (k).

## KSA applicability

OSHA 1926 is a US-reference standard, not the governing authority in Saudi
Arabia (site HSE is under Saudi labour law / MHRSD and the project HSE plan).
Gulf soils frequently require stricter measures than the generic Type A/B/C
model — sabkha, running sand, and high water tables can make even shallow
trenches hazardous. The project geotechnical report and approved shoring design
take precedence over these reference slopes in every case.

## How to use

- Quote the SI value for site use; cite the US original and the regulation /
  Appendix number so the basis is traceable.
- Treat the slope table as a US reference; use the geotechnical report and a
  designed shoring/shielding system for the actual excavation.
- State that this is a US-reference figure and that the project HSE plan and
  geotechnical design take precedence.
