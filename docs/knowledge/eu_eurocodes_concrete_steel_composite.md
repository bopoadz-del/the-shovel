# Eurocodes — concrete, steel and composite reference pointers

Citation-only scope map, checked 2026-11-06 (Asia/Dubai). Original descriptions;
no standard text, technical clause summaries, formulae or design values.
See the [basis/action map](eu_eurocodes_basis_and_actions.md) for generation,
BS EN, National Annex and applicability boundaries. The first-generation tables
below identify older base publications, not a current all-edition design suite.

## EN 1992 — concrete

This family addresses structural concrete, including reinforced and prestressed
work. Its role is material/member design alongside the common basis and actions;
it is not an alternative name for ACI 318 [C1].

| First-generation citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| EN 1992-1-1:2004 | General concrete-member and building design | Main material-design entry |
| EN 1992-1-2:2004 | Concrete members in fire situations | Concrete-specific fire reference |
| EN 1992-2:2005 | Concrete bridge design and detailing | Bridge-specific extension in the older map |
| EN 1992-3:2006 | Concrete containment and liquid-retaining works | Containment-specific extension in the older map |

The JRC part map is the source for this table [C1]. Additional verified pointers:

| Citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| BS EN 1992-4:2018 | Fastenings transferring actions into concrete [C2] | Connection/anchorage design reference; not a general prestressing part |
| BS EN 1992-1-1:2023 | Newer general concrete scope includes bridges and other civil structures [C3] | Second-generation organization; do not assume the old bridge/containment split carries over unchanged |

## EN 1994 — steel/concrete composite work

This family addresses members in which steel and concrete act together. Its place
is alongside EN 1992 and EN 1993, rather than replacing either material family.
The JRC verifies these first-generation parts and the material interfaces [M1].

| Citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| EN 1994-1-1:2004 | Composite members and buildings | Main composite-design entry |
| EN 1994-1-2:2005 | Composite members in fire situations | Composite-specific fire reference |
| EN 1994-2:2005 | Steel/concrete composite bridges | Composite bridge reference |

## Application boundary

Adopted national edition, National Annex, amendment set and compatibility of
referenced parts: **confirm against the official code**. No capacities, section
limits, factors, connection dimensions, material grades or fire ratings are
asserted by this citation map. Execution and product conformity are separate
reference areas; these design-family pointers are not fabrication acceptance rules.
