# Eurocodes — geotechnical and seismic reference pointers

Citation-only scope map, checked 2026-11-06 (Asia/Dubai). No requirements,
calculations, ground parameters or seismic values are reproduced. These are
original descriptions. See the [basis/action map](eu_eurocodes_basis_and_actions.md)
for national adoption, BS EN and generation boundaries.

## EN 1997 — ground engineering

EN 1997 occupies the ground/structure design interface. EN 1998 supplies the
seismic interface rather than EN 1997 being a stand-alone earthquake code [G1].

| First-generation citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| EN 1997-1:2004 | Common geotechnical design framework | Ground-engineering design entry |
| EN 1997-2:2007 | Investigation of the ground and associated testing | Ground-information entry in the older organization |

The first-generation table comes from the JRC [G1]. The following are separately
verified second-generation British publications, not extra parts to append to
that older design set:

| Citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| BS EN 1997-1:2024 | Newer common geotechnical design framework [G2] | General layer of the reorganized family |
| BS EN 1997-2:2024 | Characterization of ground properties [G3] | Ground-information/property layer |
| BS EN 1997-3:2025 | Design of geotechnical structures [G4] | Structure-specific layer added to the reorganized family |

The newer part -2 has a different scope description from the older investigation
and testing citation. Part -3 is verified by the publisher; it is not an inferred
identifier or a renamed copy of the older part -2.

## EN 1998 — earthquake interface

The family addresses earthquake-related structural topics in conjunction with
the other Eurocodes. Its first-generation organization is verified by JRC [E1].

| Citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| EN 1998-1:2004 | Shared seismic topics, action characterization and buildings | Main seismic entry in the older organization |
| EN 1998-2:2005 | Earthquake design of bridges | Bridge-specific seismic interface |
| EN 1998-3:2005 | Building assessment and seismic upgrading | Existing-building interface |
| EN 1998-4:2006 | Earthquake design of silos, tanks and pipelines | Storage/process-structure interface |
| EN 1998-5:2004 | Seismic aspects of foundations and ground-retaining work | Ground-engineering interface |
| EN 1998-6:2005 | Earthquake design of towers, masts and chimneys | Tall/specialist-structure interface |

### Verified newer split of the general/building topic

| Citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| BS EN 1998-1-1:2024 | Shared earthquake-design basis and seismic action topics [E2] | General seismic layer |
| BS EN 1998-1-2:2026 | Earthquake-related design of buildings [E3] | Building-specific layer |

The newer -1-2 is a building part, not an assumed structural-fire part. Its number
must not be interpreted using the fire-part convention of material Eurocodes.
This is a selected navigation map, not the complete second-generation catalogue.

## Application boundary

Applicable national edition, annex and revision set; local seismic mapping;
and compatibility with material/ground parts: **confirm against the official
code**. No soil classes, investigation spacing, bearing resistances, spectra,
return periods, behaviour factors or retrofit thresholds are asserted here.
