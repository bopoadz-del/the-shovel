# UAE Fire and Life Safety Code — evidence-backed coordination pointers

An independently authored overview of fire/life-safety coordination. The
**2018 UAE Fire and Life Safety Code of Practice** is expressly referenced by
Dubai Municipality's official **DBC 2021**. Sources checked on **2026-11-06**.

**Evidence limitation:** the DCD-indexed 2018 code page was identifiable, but its
primary code download could not be retrieved successfully for this review. The
clause pointers below were verified **indirectly through DBC 2021**, not through
a full reading of the 2018 fire-code text. They are useful entry points, not a
complete fire-code checklist or a declaration that the 2018 edition has no later
amendments. Do not substitute the accessible older 2011 code for the 2018 text
or transfer its numbering. [Sources 1–2]

## How to use these pointers safely

**Start with classification and strategy.** Establish the use, occupants, building
configuration, external exposures, and fire-service approach before selecting
individual systems. Egress, fire-resisting construction, detection, suppression,
communications, emergency lighting, and smoke control are coordinated measures;
one does not automatically remove the others. The table locates review subjects,
not the unreviewed exceptions or design thresholds. [Source 1, B.4/B.6/B.7,
E.10, H.4, H.7, H.9]

**Separate product evidence from assembly acceptance.** A material classification,
a tested assembly, the proposed fixing/joint/penetration details, and the installed
work are different evidence. A façade change needs examination against the
applicable test configuration and authority approval, not only its catalogue
label. [Source 1, E.10.4–E.10.8]

**Do not universalize Dubai-specific modifications.** DBC E.10 and H.9 contain
Dubai treatments alongside their fire-code references. Their existence is not
evidence that the same treatment, exemption, or alternative is approved in Abu
Dhabi or another emirate. Current applicability and authority acceptance:
**confirm against the official code**. [Source 1]

**Treat local amendments as jurisdiction-specific evidence.** A publicly available
consultancy-hosted copy describes Abu Dhabi Circular 3/2026 and labels its English
translation unofficial. This review did not authenticate that circular against an
authority-hosted original. No relaxation, numerical change, or exemption from it
is adopted here. The authoritative circular, Arabic/English interpretation,
effective scope, and relationship to existing approvals: **confirm against the official code** with ADCDA. [Source 3]

Design approval and construction/operational acceptance remain separate questions.
Use the [authority/NOC summary](uae_authority_noc_workflow.md) for their procedural
interfaces; a generic fire-code overview cannot establish the approvals needed
for every building.
