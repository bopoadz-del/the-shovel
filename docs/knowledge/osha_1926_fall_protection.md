# Fall Protection — Site Safety Criteria (OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M)

REAL, sourced fall-protection thresholds for construction sites. Source: OSHA
29 CFR 1926.502 (Subpart M), US federal regulation, public domain. This is a
US-reference standard: in Saudi Arabia site HSE is governed by Saudi labour law
(MHRSD) and the project's approved HSE plan, not OSHA — but Saudi practice
broadly aligns with these figures. Use as an engineering reference; enforce
against the project HSE plan. Values are metric-led (SI) with the original
US-customary figure, as written in the regulation, in parentheses.

## Guardrail systems

| Parameter | Value (SI) | Original (US) |
|---|---|---|
| Top-rail height above walking surface | 1.1 m (± 8 cm) | 42 in (± 3 in) |
| Top-rail minimum strength (force withstood) | 890 N | 200 lbf |
| Maximum opening / midrail gap | 0.48 m | 19 in |
| Toeboard minimum vertical height | 9 cm | 3½ in |

Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.502(b), (j).

## Personal fall arrest system (PFAS)

| Parameter | Value (SI) | Original (US) |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum arresting force on the body (harness) | 8 kN | 1,800 lbf |
| Maximum free-fall distance | 1.8 m | 6 ft |
| Maximum deceleration distance | 1.07 m | 3½ ft |
| Anchorage strength, per attached worker | 22.2 kN | 5,000 lbf |

Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.502(d). Note: anchorage may instead be part of a system
designed and used under a qualified person's supervision at a safety factor of 2.

## Hole and opening covers

Covers over holes must be capable of supporting at least twice the combined
weight of workers, equipment, and materials that may be imposed on them (rated to
2x the imposed load), and must be secured and colour-coded or marked "HOLE" /
"COVER".

Source: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.502(i).

## KSA applicability

OSHA 1926 is US federal site-safety law and is not the governing authority in
Saudi Arabia. Site HSE in KSA is regulated under Saudi labour law (MHRSD) and the
project's approved HSE/safety plan; SBC (Saudi Building Code) governs building
design, not site safety. These thresholds are a US engineering reference. Saudi
practice broadly aligns: guardrails ~1.0–1.1 m high, a 1.8 m free-fall limit, and
PFAS anchorage in the 22 kN range. Where a value is enforced on site, confirm it
against the project HSE plan.

## How to use

- Fall protection is generally required at a fall height of 1.8 m (6 ft) or more
  in construction (Subpart M general trigger, 1926.501).
- Quote the SI value for site use; cite the US original and the regulation number
  so the basis is traceable.
- State that this is a US-reference figure and that the project HSE plan /
  local regulation takes precedence where they differ.
- Do not present OSHA as the legal authority for a Saudi project — it is a
  reference standard only.
