# Al Sa'fat — Dubai green-building requirements and evidence

Independently authored guidance based on **Al Sa'fat, second edition, January
2023**, publicly published by Dubai Municipality. Sources checked on
**2026-11-06**. References are the article identifiers in that edition. This is
not a reproduction of its tables or a complete certification specification.
Check later circulars and the permitting authority's accepted requirements.
[Sources 1–2]

## Rating, applicability, and compliance routes

Dubai Municipality describes **Silver Sa'fa as the mandatory baseline for new
buildings**; Golden and Platinum involve additional performance requirements.
That does not make every listed article applicable to every building: the edition's
rating schedules and article-specific conditions distinguish typologies and
optional items, including villa cases. Treat the baseline, the chosen higher
rating, and the applicability of each article as separate decisions. [Sources
1–2; Source 1, Silver/Golden/Platinum requirement schedules]

- **201.01:** classify the building use before making an applicability matrix.
  In mixed-use development, assess the relevant portions by their use; a change
  of use can change the applicable green-building requirements.
- **201.02:** referenced standards enter the requirements only to the extent
  specified. Resolve a difference using the local regulation's stated treatment,
  not an assumed international-standard override.
- **201.03:** the energy routes distinguish element-by-element compliance from
  a model-based comparison. The alternative is limited to the provisions covered
  by that route; it is not a waiver of all other requirements. The reference and
  proposed cases need comparable building geometry and operation, with local
  assumptions retained rather than an unmodified generic baseline.
- **201.04:** the water-performance route concerns comparison with the prescribed
  fixture-based reference case. It does not establish permission to offset unsafe
  water arrangements with savings elsewhere. [Source 1]

## Site, comfort, and health

| Articles | Own-word summary and review focus |
|---|---|
| **301.01–301.04** | Coordinate inclusive access, preferred parking, vehicle charging, and cycling facilities with the applicable typology and rating. |
| **302.01; 304.01–304.07; 305.01** | Consider planting, heat exposure, heat-rejection location, roofs, orientation, external surfaces, shade, and environmental assessment as related site decisions. |
| **401.01–401.11** | Address ventilation and contaminant sources during both construction and use; inlet/exhaust location, commissioning, cleaning, smoking control, and parking air quality are not interchangeable checks. |
| **402.01; 403.01–403.03** | Review thermal conditions and noise/vibration transfer, including equipment interfaces, rather than treating comfort as air temperature alone. |
| **404.01–404.03; 405.01–405.02** | Connect interior-material emissions with daylight and views; certification of one material does not establish whole-room indoor-air quality. |
| **406.01–406.02; 407.01–407.03** | Include water-system hygiene, construction disturbance, execution quality, and concrete sustainability in the evidence plan. |

These are subject summaries, not a declaration that every article applies to every
rating/typology. [Source 1]

## Energy, water, materials, and closeout

- **501.01–501.06:** assess the envelope using assembly performance, thermal
  bridges, design conditions, entrance losses, air leakage, and shading; insulation
  thickness alone does not establish compliance.
- **502.01–502.13:** coordinate HVAC efficiency with demand control, lighting,
  controls, recovery, insulation, and duct leakage. Higher-rating additions and
  building-use conditions need to be checked separately.
- **503.01–503.08:** carry commissioning, metering, control-system information,
  performance reporting, and user awareness into handover planning. A design
  calculation is not evidence of installed-system performance.
- **504.01–504.03; 505.01–505.03:** assess applicable renewable-generation and
  demand-reduction provisions as part of the whole energy strategy, not as
  automatic substitutes for the chosen compliance route.
- **601.01–601.04; 602.01; 603.01–603.02:** distinguish efficient fixtures,
  condensate drainage/recovery, irrigation, metering, wastewater reuse, and
  cooling-water issues. Reuse needs suitable treatment, distribution, and water
  quality, not just a calculated reduction in potable demand.
- **701.01–701.08; 702.01–702.05:** establish product/material evidence and
  construction/operational waste arrangements. The source's notes make reuse
  conditions and the rating/typology schedules important to applicability.
  [Source 1]

Use a generic evidence register linking each applicable article to its design
calculation, specification, product evidence, inspection/test record, and closeout
record. This is an author-recommended coordination method, not a claim that a
particular spreadsheet or submission template is prescribed by the code.
