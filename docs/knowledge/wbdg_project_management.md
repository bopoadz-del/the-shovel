# Construction Project Management — Delivery Methods & Phases (WBDG)

Reference guidance on construction project delivery and project management.
Source: WBDG Project Management. This is best-practice guidance (how the industry
runs projects), not a regulation or a binding standard — the project's own contract
and the client's procedures govern any specific project. International practice, so
it applies to KSA projects directly. Grounds the platform's WBS, schedule, and cost
tooling.

## Project delivery methods

| Method | How it works | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Design-Bid-Build (DBB) | Design fully completed, then competitively bid, then built; owner holds separate design and construction contracts | Clear, price-competitive; slower (sequential), more change/claim risk |
| Design-Build (DB) | One contract covers both design and construction | Faster, single point of responsibility; less owner control of detail |
| Construction Manager at Risk (CMAR) | CM engaged early as advisor, then holds construction risk under a Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP) | Early collaboration + cost certainty; relies on GMP trust |
| Integrated Project Delivery (IPD) | Multi-party contract with shared risk/reward and early collaboration | Highest integration; needs mature, aligned teams |
| Fast-track | Construction starts before design is fully complete (phases overlap) | Compresses schedule; higher rework/change risk |

Source: WBDG Project Management — delivery methods.

## Project phases

| Phase | Focus | Key output |
|---|---|---|
| Planning / Programming | Match owner needs to budget and scope | Programmatic requirements; budget; delivery method chosen |
| Schematic Design (SD) | Concept, massing, major systems | Schematic drawings; preliminary estimate |
| Design Development (DD) | Systems and materials resolved | Developed design; refined estimate |
| Construction Documents (CD) | Full drawings and specifications | Biddable/permittable document set |
| Procurement / Bidding | Select the contractor | Award; contract |
| Construction | Build, administer, inspect | Completed works; progress claims |
| Commissioning & Closeout | Verify, hand over, document | Systems manual; as-builts; final account |

Source: WBDG Project Management — project phases.

## Cost and estimating discipline

- Preliminary estimates in early planning match the owner's programmatic
  requirements to the budget, setting scope (size) and quality before design.
- Value engineering is applied across the life cycle — best value, not lowest
  first cost, judged against whole-life cost.

Source: WBDG — Utilize Cost and Value Engineering Throughout the Project Life Cycle.

## How to use

- Use this to explain delivery-method choice, the phase sequence, and where cost
  control and value engineering fit.
- The client's contract, procurement rules, and internal procedures are
  authoritative for a specific project — this is the general framework.
- The phase and delivery structure informs WBS build-up and schedule logic; keep
  computed quantities and durations from the deterministic tools, not from prose.
