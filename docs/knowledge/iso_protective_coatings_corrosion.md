# ISO — steel paint protection and coating reference pointers

Citation-only navigation, checked 2026-11-06 (Asia/Dubai). All descriptions are
original subject pointers. No standard clauses, exposure categories, durability
bands, coating thicknesses, preparation grades, test conditions or acceptance
values are reproduced. ISO's protective-paint committee catalogue verifies every
identifier and scope division listed below [C1].

## ISO 12944 — paint protection of structural steel

The series sits at the corrosion-protection specification/execution interface.
It does not replace steel structural design under EN 1993 or automatically apply
to every substrate and protection technology. These pointers describe where to
look, not how to select or approve a coating system.

| Citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |

The catalogue lists developing revisions alongside several published parts.
A WD or CD entry is not a published replacement. The identifiers above identify
published editions; they are not predictions of future revision years [C1].

## Application boundary

Adopted edition and amendments, substrate and exposure applicability, required
coating system, inspection method and acceptance criteria: **confirm against the
official code** or standard and the governing specification. No service-life
promise, manufacturer's product approval, inspection interval or pass/fail value
can be derived from this directory.
