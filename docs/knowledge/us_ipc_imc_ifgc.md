# US building services — IPC, IMC, and IFGC

Original summaries of **2021 IPC, IMC, and IFGC**. Public publisher contents verify
the navigation entries below; public technical/authority sources support the
specific examples. Checked **2026-11-06**. Determine the adopted code family and
local amendments first: IPC and UPC, for example, are not interchangeable names
for the same document. Manufacturer/listing instructions and utility requirements
also need coordination. [Sources 1–6]

## IPC — water quality, drainage, and usable facilities

Verified review entry points: **403** for fixture provision; **604** for water
supply design; **608** for potable-water protection; **704/709** for drainage
installation/loading; **906** for vent sizing; **1002** for traps; **1106** for
stormwater sizing; and **312** for tests/inspections. A drainage fixture-unit
value is not automatically a water-supply sizing value. Venting, drainage, and
backflow protection solve different problems. [Source 1]

**Resolved slope example, 704.1/Table 704.1:** ordinary horizontal drainage
minimums are **¼ in/ft** for pipe up to **2½ in**, **⅛ in/ft** for **3–6 in**,
and **1/16 in/ft** for **8 in and larger**. Piping upstream of a grease
interceptor instead needs at least **¼ in/ft**. Retain the stated uniformity,
applicability, and adopted-code conditions. This is not a pipe-capacity calculation
or a UPC table. [Sources 4–5]

For a coordinated review, separate public-water connection/backflow requirements,
fixture counts and accessibility, hot-water safety, drainage/vent arrangements,
cleanouts, storm overflow, and installation tests. Do not accept a product listing
as proof that its location, connections, and system arrangement comply.
[Source 1, cited subject sections]

## IMC — ventilation, exhaust, fire interfaces, and maintenance access

Use **306–307** to coordinate service space and condensate disposal. **403**
addresses mechanical ventilation; **501–502** exhaust generally; **506–507**
kitchen exhaust/hoods; **510** hazardous exhaust; and **607** duct/transfer
openings in fire-related construction. Refrigeration is addressed in **Chapter 11**.
A comfort-airflow calculation does not resolve contaminant capture, grease duct
construction, refrigerant hazards, or fire/smoke separation. [Source 2]

**Resolved ventilation example:** the **Table 403.3.1.1 office-space row** uses
**5 cfm/person plus 0.06 cfm/ft²** for breathing-zone outdoor air, with a listed
density of **5 persons/1,000 ft²**. Both people and area contributions matter;
conference rooms have different density assumptions. The table result is not
necessarily the final outdoor intake of a multizone air handler: distribution,
system calculation, operating controls, exhaust/makeup, and applicability remain
to be addressed. Corroborated by a public 2021 code training extract and publicly
published adopted text. [Sources 6–7]

## IFGC — demand, installation, combustion air, and verification

Use **304–305** for combustion-air/appliance installation issues; **402–404**
for sizing, material, and installation; **406** for test/inspection/purge;
**409** for shutoffs; and **503** for appliance venting. Pipe diameter cannot be
selected from appliance name alone: gas properties, available pressure, allowable
loss, developed/equivalent length, and demand matter. Do not equate room
ventilation with a complete combustion-air or flue design. [Source 3]

**Resolved pressure-test example, 406.4.1:** the test pressure is at least
**1.5 times** the proposed maximum working pressure and at least **3 psig**.
**406.4.2** uses **30 minutes per 500 ft³** of pipe volume or fraction, with a
**10-minute** minimum for systems below **10 ft³** or single-family dwellings;
the specified duration need not exceed **24 hours**.
Use the approved test medium, gauge, isolation, duration, and leak/purge procedure;
a pressure value alone is not a safe commissioning method. [Sources 8–10]

## Remaining governing-code decisions

The adopted editions, water/utility criteria, actual fixture/occupancy loads,
selected materials and system conditions, equipment listings, and approval of
alternatives are not specified here: **confirm against the official code** and
relevant authority/manufacturer requirements. The worked reference values above
were researched, not left as unsourced constants. No universal pipe-size,
ventilation, gas-test, or pressure table is invented for unspecified conditions.
