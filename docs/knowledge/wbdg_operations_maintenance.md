# Operations & Maintenance — Handover, Manuals & Strategy (WBDG)

Reference guidance on facility operations and maintenance (O&M) and the
construction-to-operations handover. Source: WBDG Operations & Maintenance, framed
on ASHRAE Standard 202. Best-practice guidance, not a regulation — the project's
O&M requirements and the client's facility-management procedures govern. Bridges
the commissioning Systems Manual (see the commissioning note) to facility
operations, and pairs with the BIM/COBie note for structured data handover.

## What O&M covers

O&M is the full set of services that keep the built asset available and performing
the function it was designed and built for — through its operating life, from
handover onward.

Source: WBDG Operations & Maintenance.

## Maintenance strategies

| Strategy | Basis | When it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Reactive (run-to-failure) | Fix on breakdown | Non-critical, cheap-to-replace items |
| Preventive | Scheduled by time or usage | Most building systems; predictable wear |
| Predictive (condition-based) | Monitored condition triggers action | High-value / critical equipment |
| Reliability-centered (RCM) | Failure-mode analysis sets the strategy per asset | Complex, mission-critical facilities |

Source: WBDG O&M practice (industry maintenance strategy hierarchy).

## The handover

- Early involvement by the Commissioning Provider is critical so the Owner's
  Project Requirements (OPR), Basis of Design (BOD), Commissioning Plan, and the
  O&M Systems Manual develop together, not as an afterthought.
- At substantial completion, after O&M training, the Owner assumes operational
  responsibility. Commissioning is not complete until off-season testing is done
  and the systems manual is transferred to the Owner.

Source: WBDG Building Commissioning / O&M handover.

## Systems / O&M Manual contents

Per ASHRAE Standard 202-2018 and Guideline 1.4-2019, the Systems Manual includes:
the OPR and BOD, submittals, as-built drawings, specifications, certifications, a
Facility Guide (building operating parameters), operational and maintenance
requirements and O&M manuals, training documents, and commissioning documentation
(test/verification reports, final issue-resolution logs).

Source: ASHRAE Standard 202-2018; Guideline 1.4-2019 (via WBDG).

## COBie handover

COBie (Construction-Operations Building Information Exchange) is the structured
delivery of asset information — equipment, spaces, spares, warranties — from the
construction team to facility management, in a spreadsheet/IFC form. It replaces
hand-transcribing hundreds of pages of paper O&M manuals, so the FM system is
populated from the project data directly. See the BIM reference note.

Source: WBDG — Construction-Operations Building Information Exchange (COBie).

## How to use

- Use this to explain O&M scope, maintenance strategy choice, the handover
  sequence, and what the Systems Manual / O&M Manual must contain.
- The client's FM procedures and the project O&M specification are authoritative
  for a specific project — this is the general framework.
