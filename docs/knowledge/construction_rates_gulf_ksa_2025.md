# Construction Cost Benchmarks — Saudi Arabia & Gulf (2025)

REAL, sourced construction cost benchmarks for Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. Use
these as the GENERAL-KNOWLEDGE reference when the project has no priced BOQ of its
own. Always cite the source and year, and state that a benchmark figure is
indicative — a supplier quote or the client's own rate schedule takes precedence.

Currency: SAR (Saudi Riyal); USD conversions approximate (~3.75 SAR/USD).

## How to use

- These are REAL benchmark figures for the Gulf/KSA region — prefer them over any
  generic global estimate when the project is in this region.
- The project's own priced BOQ / rate schedule (uploaded to the project) is still
  authoritative and takes precedence over these benchmarks.
- Ranges reflect grade/spec/market variation — quote the range, cite the source
  and year, and recommend a supplier quote for a firm price.
- Do not sum a material-supply rate with an all-in build-cost/m² rate — they are
  different bases.
