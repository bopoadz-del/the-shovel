# US accessibility — ADA 2010 and ICC A117.1-2017 are distinct obligations

Original summary of the **2010 ADA Standards** and selected **ICC A117.1-2017**
technical provisions. Official federal material and ICC public samples were
checked **2026-11-06**. The 2017 ICC edition is selected because it is referenced
by the 2021 IBC, not because every jurisdiction uses it or it supersedes federal
ADA rules. [Sources 1–5]

## Separate legal coverage, scoping, and technical detail

DOJ's assembled standards explain Title II state/local government facilities and
Title III public accommodations/commercial facilities. Their regulations, including
**28 CFR 35.151** and **28 CFR Part 36, Subpart D**, work with the technical
standards. New construction, alterations, programme accessibility, and existing
barrier removal are not interchangeable legal tests. A building-code approval is
not a determination that every federal civil-rights obligation has been met.
[Source 1]

Within the technical standards, **Chapter 2** establishes which elements and how
many are required; **Chapters 3–10** address their characteristics. A117.1 supplies
technical criteria when invoked by the adopting code; the adoption supplies
scoping. Neither a dimension checklist nor an A117.1 dwelling-unit designation
by itself establishes the full extent of ADA coverage. [Sources 1, 3–5]

## Publicly resolved dimensions — keep the edition and context attached

| Subject | Selected provision | Own-word numerical summary and qualification |
|---|---|---|
| ADA walking surface | **403.5.1** | Generally **36 in (915 mm)** clear; the specifically limited narrowing exception is not a licence for a continuous 32-in route. Turns and passing spaces have additional provisions. |
| ADA manual door | **404.2.3** | Generally **32 in (815 mm)** clear, measured for the actual door opening rather than nominal leaf size; openings deeper than **24 in (610 mm)** need **36 in (915 mm)** clear. Other door clearances/hardware still apply. |
| ADA ramp | **405.2** | Normal maximum running slope **1:12**; the steeper existing-site exception is tightly limited by rise/space conditions, not a general alternative design slope. |
| ADA circular turn | **304.3.1** | **60 in (1525 mm)** diameter minimum; a qualifying T-shaped arrangement is a separate option. |
| ADA clear floor space | **305.3** | Basic minimum rectangle **30 × 48 in (760 × 1220 mm)**; approach, recess, and knee/toe conditions can add constraints. |
| A117.1-2017 circular turn | **304.3.1.1 / 304.3.1.2** | New-building/facility circle **67 in (1700 mm)**; existing-building/facility circle **60 in (1525 mm)**. Use the correct category and overlap conditions. |
| A117.1-2017 clear floor space | **305.3.1 / 305.3.2** | New-building/facility basic rectangle **30 × 52 in (760 × 1320 mm)**; existing-building/facility **30 × 48 in (760 × 1220 mm)**. This is not an exemption from the rest of the adopted provisions. |

ADA values: Sources 1–2. ICC values: Sources 3–4. The larger 2017 ICC dimensions
do not silently amend the federal 2010 standards; where both apply, coordinate
both. Units are the publications' displayed nominal equivalents, not extra
construction tolerances.

## Review the continuous usable journey

Coordinate site arrival, parking/loading, route, entrance, internal circulation,
vertical access, toilets, service counters, communication features, and evacuation
interfaces. Assess door approach/swing/force/hardware as well as opening width;
assess reach, protrusions, surface changes, turning and transfers as well as room
area. The dimension of one compliant element does not establish usable access
to it. [Sources 1–4, Chapter 2 and relevant technical chapters]

## Remaining applicability

Covered entity/use, work timing/type, existing-facility duties, adopted ICC edition,
scoping exceptions, and any equivalent-facilitation basis are unspecified:
**confirm against the official code** and applicable federal regulations. The
cited dimensions are publicly sourced; no facility-specific count or exemption
is invented.
