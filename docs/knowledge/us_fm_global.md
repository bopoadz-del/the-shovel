# FM Global / FM — property-loss-prevention guidance and approval interfaces

Original review of selected publicly available FM Property Loss Prevention Data
Sheets, checked **2026-11-06**. These are insurer/loss-prevention recommendations,
not automatically statutory building-code requirements. An insurance or contract
condition may invoke them separately. The revisions actually reviewed are
identified below; an accessible older PDF is not proof of the latest FM revision.
[Sources 1–6]

## Selected, source-verified review locations

| Data sheet and reviewed revision | Own-word scope and numbered pointers |
|---|---|
| **1-28**, October 2015 / interim **April 2026** | **1.0** covers relevant wind-related components/cladding and equipment, not the complete main structural wind-resisting system. **2.1** addresses design pressures; **2.4** opening protection; **2.6** roof equipment; **2.7** wind-tunnel treatment; **2.11** tornadoes. |
| **1-29**, January 2016 / interim **July 2022** | **2.2** addresses roof construction/securement; **2.2.3** steel deck; **2.2.10** above-deck components; **2.3** inspection/maintenance. Coordinate the selected assembly and its actual substrate, fasteners/adhesives, zones, and installation details. |
| **2-0**, October 2021 / interim **January 2024** | **2.1** locates general sprinkler/water-supply interfaces; **2.2** system types; **2.4** piping/supports; **2.5** sprinklers and obstructions/clearances; **2.6** drawings, hydraulics, specifications, and review/acceptance evidence. |
| **7-29**, October 2020 / interim **April 2026** | Portable-container liquid storage within **1.0** scope: **2.2.2** containment/drainage; **2.3** occupancy/ventilation; **2.4** arrangement-specific protection; **2.5** operation; **2.8** ignition control. It is not a universal bulk-tank or unstable-liquid protection standard. |

Sources 1–4 establish those scopes and section references. For 2-0, the FM-hosted
copy reviewed is a **May 2024 Spanish translation** of the January 2024 revision;
Source 5 is an engineering-service-hosted copy of the English original, whose
cover and section text were also reviewed. NBS's public index lists the reviewed 2022 **1-29** as current at this check; that
secondary evidence does not establish the currency of **2-0**. Do not present a
reviewed revision as a newer document. [Source 6]

## Resolved examples and edition changes

**Sprinkler clearance:** the reviewed 2-0 **2.5.1.10.1–2.5.1.10.2** distinguish
**3 ft (0.9 m)** below standard-coverage ceiling-level deflectors and **5 ft
(1.5 m)** below extended-coverage ceiling-level deflectors for the described
combustible-clearance cases. These are FM revision-specific recommendations,
not a replacement for every adopted NFPA provision or a design for every
obstruction/storage configuration. [Sources 4–5]

**Liquid classification:** the April 2026 7-29 **1.0/1.2** revision record moves
classification guidance to **Data Sheet 7-32A**. Do not keep using a removed older
7-29 classification table as though it were the current classification basis.
Container material/capacity, liquid behaviour, storage arrangement, and protection
method must match the applicable case in **2.4**. [Source 3]

**Roof selection:** the 1-28 pressure calculation and the 1-29 assembly/securement
selection solve different parts of the design. A product approval or roof rating
alone is not the site wind demand, and a collection of individually approved
components does not automatically establish an approved roof assembly. Edge/
corner conditions, openings, deck/support suitability, and installation evidence
remain part of the review. [Sources 1–2]

## Remaining applicability and revision control

The insurer's accepted design basis, latest applicable revisions, selected
approval listing/assembly, actual site demand, storage hazards, and accepted
alternatives are unspecified: **confirm against the official code** and FM's
applicable requirements. The reviewed public documents resolve the cited values
and pointers; a newer accepted 2-0 revision is not inferred from an older public link. No universal roof fastening schedule or sprinkler storage density
is invented.
