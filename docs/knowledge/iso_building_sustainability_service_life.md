# ISO — construction sustainability and service-life reference pointers

Citation-only directory, checked 2026-11-06 (Asia/Dubai). Original scope locators;
no copied standard text, clauses, environmental factors, service-life values,
financial assumptions or assessment procedures. Citation years and part numbers
are bibliographic metadata only.

## Environmental assessment and construction declarations

| Citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits / source |
|---|---|---|
| ISO 14040:2006; Amd 1:2020 | Concepts and framework for life-cycle assessment | General LCA foundation [L1] |
| ISO 14044:2006; Amd 1:2017; Amd 2:2020 | LCA method/assessment requirements and guidance | Companion methodological reference; procedures are not reproduced here [L1] |
| ISO 14025:2026 | Environmental declaration programmes and associated product declarations | General EPD reference; not a building rating scheme [L2] |
| ISO 15392:2019 | Sustainability concepts for buildings and civil works | Construction-sector orientation [S1] |
| ISO 21930:2017 | Construction-product/service-specific EPD rules | Sector interface between environmental declarations and construction products [S1] |
| ISO 21931-1:2022 | Sustainability-assessment frameworks for buildings | Building-scale assessment-method context [S1] |
| ISO 21931-2:2019 | Sustainability-assessment frameworks for civil works | Civil-infrastructure counterpart [S1] |
| ISO 20887:2020 | Design addressing future disassembly and adaptation | Design-for-change reference [S1] |
| ISO 22057:2022 | Templates connecting construction-product EPD information with BIM | Environmental-data / digital-information interface [S1] |

ISO 14025 is listed with its published 2026 edition, not its withdrawn predecessor
[L2]. The construction-specific ISO 21930 record is separately identified [S1].
This is not a claim that particular declarations or programmes meet either
standard. Organizational environmental management belongs in
[ISO 14001's separate reference group](iso_management_systems_risk_assets.md),
not in place of product LCA or an EPD.

## Selected ISO 15686 service-life planning parts

ISO's service-life committee catalogue verifies these published parts [D1].
They occupy the durability/asset-life and economic-appraisal interface, not a
source of universal design-life promises.

| Citation | Coverage pointer | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| ISO 15686-1:2011 | Overall service-life planning framework | Entry to the published series |
| ISO 15686-2:2012 | Procedures for predicting service life | Prediction-method reference |
| ISO 15686-3:2002 | Performance audits and reviews in service-life planning | Evaluation/review interface |
| ISO 15686-4:2014 | Service-life planning linked with BIM | Digital-information interface |
| ISO 15686-5:2017 | Cost appraisal over an asset's life | Life-cycle-cost reference, distinct from environmental LCA |
| ISO 15686-7:2017 | Feedback of in-use performance into service-life information | Operational-evidence interface |
| ISO 15686-8:2008 | Reference-life information and life estimation | Service-life data/estimation reference |

The catalogue also contains developing FDIS revisions to parts -1, -2 and -3.
Their proposed scope divisions are not substituted for the published parts
above [D1]. This is a selected construction-relevant set, not a claim to cover
every ISO sustainability standard or every part of ISO 15686.

## Application boundary

Applicable standard/adoption, amendments, declaration programme and assessment
scope: **confirm against the official code** or standard and the governing
assessment framework. No carbon factors, comparison thresholds, discount rates,
replacement periods, reference service lives or sustainability scores are
asserted. No ISO citation here establishes a local green-building approval.
